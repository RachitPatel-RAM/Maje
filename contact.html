Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a typical project take?

Project timelines vary depending on complexity and scope. Simple websites typically take 2-4 weeks, while complex applications can take 3-6 months. We provide detailed timelines during the planning phase.

Do you provide ongoing support?

Yes! We offer comprehensive maintenance and support packages to ensure your project continues to perform optimally. This includes updates, security patches, and technical support.

What technologies do you use?

We use modern, industry-standard technologies including React, Node.js, Python, AI/ML frameworks, and cloud platforms like AWS and Google Cloud to deliver scalable solutions.

Can you work with our existing team?

Absolutely! We can integrate seamlessly with your existing team, providing expertise in specific areas or taking on the entire project. We're flexible in our collaboration approach.

What's included in your pricing?

Our pricing includes development, testing, deployment, and initial support. We provide transparent pricing with no hidden fees and detailed project breakdowns.

Do you offer consultation services?

Yes! We provide strategic consultation to help you plan your digital transformation, choose the right technologies, and develop a roadmap for success.
